Mean, Median & Mode Calculator

Enter a set of numbers to find the mean, median, mode, range, and more.

Separate numbers with commas, spaces, or new lines.

Definitions

Mean: The sum of all values divided by the count.

Median: The middle value when sorted. If even count, average of two middle values.

Mode: The most frequently occurring value(s).

Range: The difference between the maximum and minimum values.

Count: The total number of values in the data set.
